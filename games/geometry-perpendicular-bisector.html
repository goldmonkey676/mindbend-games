You found the border.

Every step of the way you were building the same line — the one where the two castles tie.

That line has a name: the perpendicular bisector of segment AB. Perpendicular, because it crosses AB at a right angle. Bisector, because it cuts AB exactly in half. And the reason the compass construction works: swinging the same radius from A and from B lands you on points that are equally far from both — so the line through them is exactly this border.
1There's always a spot where the distance to A equals the distance to B.
2Those equal-distance spots aren't scattered — they line up dead straight.
3That line crosses segment AB exactly at its midpoint: AM + MB = AB, with AM = MB.
4It crosses AB at a right angle — that's the only tilt where the balance holds all along the line.
5It splits the whole map in two: every point is claimed by whichever castle is closer.
