You mapped it all out.

Four different ways distance shows up — and it was the same idea underneath every time.

1On a line, distance is just the gap between two numbers — and there are always two spots the same distance away.
2On a grid, the straight-line distance is the hypotenuse of the right triangle made from the horizontal and vertical steps.
3Every point equally far from two spots lines up perfectly straight — that line is the perpendicular bisector.
4The same triangle works at any scale — grid units convert straight into real-world distance.
